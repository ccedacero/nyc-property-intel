## 🔴 The one thing to fix: `/api/watch` has no abuse protection (HIGH)

The 1.9 watch endpoint is **unauthenticated with zero abuse defenses** — no IP rate limit, no honeypot, no Turnstile, no per-email cap. Every *other* signup endpoint on this server (`/api/signup`, `/api/chat/signup`) has layered defenses (honeypot + Turnstile + `_check_signup_ip_rate_limit`, 3/IP/hour). `/api/watch` is the visible inconsistency.

**Concrete exposure:**
- **Watch-bombing a third party** — an attacker can subscribe a *victim's* email to many buildings (each `(email, bbl)` is a distinct row), and since there's no opt-in confirmation, the victim receives a real alert email per watched building (subject to the 7-day per-watch cooldown). 
- **Loops quota + sending-domain reputation** — mass watches burn transactional-email quota and risk the domain's deliverability.

It's HIGH (not Critical) only because the 7-day cooldown caps per-watch frequency.

**Recommended fix (clean, ~all parts already exist):**
1. Reuse `_check_signup_ip_rate_limit` / `_get_client_ip` (already importable) on `/api/watch`.
2. Add a per-email cap on active watches (e.g. ≤25).
3. Ideally a double-opt-in: first watch on a new email sends a confirm link; alerts only after confirmation. (Biggest blast-radius reducer — kills third-party watch-bombing entirely.)
